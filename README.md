## How to Use the Prototype

**Platform:** FigmaAR  
**Prototype Link:** [BeyondARt](https://figma-ar-studio-kwatraprisha49.replit.app)

### Instructions
1. Open the link on either a mobile or desktop device.  
2. Allow camera access when prompted.  
3. Scan the designated gallery markers or printed artworks.  
   *(For demonstration purposes, several default reflections are already displayed.)*  
4. Explore reflections shared by previous visitors.  
5. Tap to leave your own reflection - choose whether to post **with your name** or **anonymously**.  
6. You may also use **AI-generated suggestions** or **add emojis** to express your feelings.  
7. Interact with others’ posts by **liking, commenting, or sharing** to create a sense of connection within the experience.
