## Week 10

**Discussion Points:**  
- Reviewed both AR and Figma prototypes.  
- Integrated feedback from Week 9.  
- Planned improvements for social interaction and interface design.  

**Team Notes:**  
- **Rakshitha:** Suggested making the comment background transparent so it doesn’t cover the artwork, adding more comments, and allowing users to drag and move comment bubbles.  
- **Prisha:** Proposed adding a step asking users how they want to share - *anonymously* or *with their name*.  
- **Team Suggestions:**  
  - Fix overlapping comment bubbles.  
  - Add an **AI suggestion** button for emotional reflection.  
  - Include **like** and **comment** features to enhance engagement.  
  - Enable emoji reactions for quick feedback.  
  - Encourage sharing or saving meaningful reflections.  

**Additional Feedback Discussion:**  
- Missing **“Back” button** for users to return from the comment page.  
- Suggestion to include the **artwork image** in shared posts for visual context.  
- Improve shareable card visuals for social media distribution.  
- Explore **multi-image recognition** for different artworks to trigger unique AR reflections.  
- Confirm that scanning different artworks generates distinct reflection bubbles.
